## Description

Bring your invoices, clients, and payments together in a practical workspace for freelancers, creatives, consultants, and small businesses.

**Cellspray Invoice & Payment Tracker** is a downloadable browser app. Extract the ZIP and open the included HTML file in a modern desktop browser. No Node.js, terminal commands, Excel, Google Sheets, or cloud account is required. The app runs offline, with records stored locally in your browser.

### Make invoicing a single flow

Create an invoice, choose an existing client or add a new one inside the invoice form, enter your products or services, and optionally record an initial deposit. Save once to update the client, invoice, payment, balance, and dashboard together.

### Included features

- Create, edit, duplicate, search, and filter invoices.
- Add multiple line items, discounts, tax, due dates, and payment instructions.
- Record deposits, partial payments, and final payments; balances and invoice statuses update automatically.
- Manage client details and review payment history.
- See total invoiced, collected, outstanding, overdue, monthly collections, and aging balances.
- Use your own business logo, business name, optional tagline, and invoice accent color.
- Print invoices or choose **Save as PDF** in your browser to share with clients.
- Work with USD, GBP, CAD, AUD, and EUR. Dashboard totals stay separate by currency; no exchange-rate conversion is applied.
- Download JSON backups, merge or replace a workspace, and undo the last restore.
- Choose light or dark mode and use the responsive layout.
- Explore clearly labeled sample data, then choose **Start fresh** for your own records.

Payments are recorded manually. You share the printed or saved invoice with your client yourself.

### Usage

Use your copy for your own personal and business needs, including multiple businesses you own or operate. There is no per-business fee or subscription for this downloaded version. You may share the invoices and PDFs you create with clients. Resale or redistribution of Cellspray's original app and guides is not included. Full terms and the licenses for bundled open-source components are included in the ZIP.

Each workspace uses one business identity. For multiple businesses, use separate browser profiles and backups as explained in the User Guide.

### Browser and storage details

Recommended: a current desktop version of **Chrome or Edge on Windows or Mac**, opened in a normal browser window.

Your records belong to the browser profile and device where you use the app. There is no cloud synchronization. Copying the HTML file alone does not copy your invoices, clients, or payments.

Keep the app in one folder and reopen it using the same browser profile. Storage for local HTML files can vary between browsers and after moving or renaming the app. Clearing browser data removes locally saved records; private browsing may discard them when its window closes.

**Export a JSON backup regularly**, and before moving the app, switching browsers or devices, or clearing browser data. To move your workspace, download a backup from the original browser and restore it in the new one.
