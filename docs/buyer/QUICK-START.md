# Cellspray

## Invoice & Payment Tracker — Quick Start

Create invoices, record payments, and keep your business records together in your browser. Your own business name and logo appear on customer invoices; Cellspray identifies the software.

Use the downloaded HTML app on your desktop, or open the hosted website on your phone using the link in step 2.

### 1. Download and extract

For desktop use, download the product ZIP file to your computer, then extract it into a folder you plan to keep. For phone access, continue to the mobile link in step 2.

- **Windows:** right-click the ZIP file and choose **Extract All**.
- **Mac:** double-click the ZIP file to extract its contents.

Open the extracted folder. Do not open the tracker while it is still inside the ZIP.

### 2. Open your tracker

**On your desktop:**

Double-click **Cellspray-Invoice-Payment-Tracker.html**. It opens in a web browser.

Use a current desktop version of **Chrome or Edge** on Windows or Mac for the intended workflow. If the file opens in another app, right-click it and choose **Open with**, then select your browser. Use a normal browser window.

Keep the same HTML filename, folder, browser, and browser profile for everyday use. Before moving the file, changing browsers, or installing an updated copy, download a JSON backup.

**On your phone:**

[Open the online tracker](https://cellspray-invoice-payment-tracker.umair-aa092.workers.dev/)

Open the link in a normal **Chrome window on Android** or **Safari window on iPhone**. An internet connection is needed when opening or reloading the hosted website.

If a PDF or chat app's embedded browser cannot open it, copy the link into Chrome or Safari directly. If it still fails, share the visible error text with the seller. Keep a JSON backup before clearing any browser data.

For a convenient home-screen shortcut:

- **Android Chrome:** open the browser menu, choose **Add to Home screen**, and follow the shortcut prompts.
- **iPhone Safari:** tap **Share**, choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.

Menu wording can vary. The shortcut opens the website; it does not turn it into an offline app.

### 3. Make the workspace yours

On first use, the tracker shows clearly labeled sample data. Choose **Start fresh** and review the confirmation to remove sample clients, invoices, and payments.

Open **Business settings**. Replace the sample business details with your own:

- Business name, optional tagline, email, phone, and address.
- Optional business logo: PNG, JPG, or WEBP, up to 2 MB.
- Invoice accent color, default currency, payment terms, and tax settings.
- Payment instructions for new invoices.

Select **Save settings**. Your saved branding appears automatically in your workspace and invoice previews. Change or remove your logo here whenever needed, then save again.

### 4. Create your first invoice

Select **New invoice**.

1. Choose an existing client, or select **Add new client** and enter their details inside the invoice form.
2. Check the invoice number, issue date, due date, and currency.
3. Add your products or services, quantities, and prices. Add a discount or tax if needed.
4. Optionally select **Record a payment or deposit now** and enter a payment you have already received.
5. Review the live preview, then select **Save invoice**.

One save creates the client, invoice, and optional payment together. Balances, statuses, and dashboard totals update automatically.

### 5. Record later payments and save a PDF

Use **Record payment** to select an invoice and enter an amount received. Payments are recorded manually; the tracker does not collect or transfer money.

To share an invoice on desktop, open **Invoices**, use its **Print** action, then choose **Print / Save as PDF**. Select **Save as PDF** in the browser’s print dialog and save the document. Phone print and sharing options vary by browser and device. Send the PDF to your client yourself.

### 6. Keep a backup

Open **Backup & restore** and select **Download backup**. Keep the JSON file in a separate safe folder. Repeat regularly and before moving or updating the tracker.

To bring records back, choose your JSON file and review the summary:

- **Merge** keeps current records and adds missing records from the backup. Populated current branding takes priority.
- **Replace** replaces the workspace with the backup after confirmation.
- **Undo last restore** returns to the snapshot saved before the most recent merge or replace. Changes made since that restore are removed.

To move desktop records to your phone, transfer the downloaded JSON backup file to the phone, open the mobile link, and choose that file in **Backup & restore**. Use Replace for a complete transfer into a fresh workspace, or Merge to keep records already there.

### Remember

Records are stored locally in each browser and device. The downloaded HTML file and hosted website have separate workspaces; records do not appear on your phone automatically. Clearing browser data, using private or incognito mode, or changing file locations can make saved records unavailable. There is no cloud sync. A PDF is for sharing an invoice; a JSON backup is for restoring your workspace.

The tracker supports **USD, GBP, CAD, AUD, and EUR**. Choose a dashboard currency to view its totals. Amounts in different currencies remain separate, with no exchange-rate conversion.

Read the included **User Guide** for the full workflow, restore instructions, and troubleshooting.
